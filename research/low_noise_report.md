## 2. Pre-Registered Strategy Specifications

All strategies operate on symbol-agnostic price and funding data. Indicator calculations are strictly causal ($t \le T$).

### F0: Daily Continuous Trend Score with Realized Volatility Targeting
- **Bar Timeframe:** 1-Day completed bars (resampled from 1H).
- **Trend Score:** $\text{Score}_t = \frac{1}{4} \sum_{N \in \{20, 50, 100, 200\}} \text{sign}(\text{close}_t - \text{close}_{t-N}) \in [-1.0, +1.0]$.
- **Realized Volatility:** $\sigma_{\text{realized}, t} = \text{std}(\Delta \ln \text{close}, 20\text{ days}) \times \sqrt{365}$.
- **Target Sizing:** $\text{Target Sizing} = \text{clip}\left(\text{Score}_t \times \frac{25\%}{\sigma_{\text{realized}, t}}, -2.0, +2.0\right)$.
- **Rebalancing Band:** Trade only if $|	ext{Target Sizing} - 	ext{Current Leverage}| > 25\% 	imes |	ext{Current Leverage}|$. Avoids microscopic churn.
- **F0_LO (Long-Only Variant):** Flat if $	ext{close}_t < 	ext{SMA}_{200}(t)$; otherwise $	ext{Score}_t = \max(	ext{Score}_t, 0)$.

### G0: 6-Hour Donchian Breakout + EMA(100) Trend Filter
- **Bar Timeframe:** 6-Hour completed bars (resampled from 1H).
- **Long Entry:** $	ext{close}_t > \max(	ext{close}_{t-20 \dots t-1}) 	ext{ AND } 	ext{close}_t > 	ext{EMA}_{100}(	ext{close}_t)$.
- **Short Entry:** $	ext{close}_t < \min(	ext{close}_{t-20 \dots t-1}) 	ext{ AND } 	ext{close}_t < 	ext{EMA}_{100}(	ext{close}_t)$.
- **Sizing:** Long sizing targets 1% equity risk / $(3 \times \text{ATR}_{14})$. Short notional = $30\% \times \text{Long Notional}$. Maximum gross leverage capped at 2.0x.
- **Exit Engine:** ATR trailing stop placed at $3 \times \text{ATR}_{14}$ from the highest close (longs) or lowest close (shorts) since entry. The stop is strictly monotonic (ratchets only, never loosens).
- **Cooldown:** 4 completed 6H bars (24 hours) enforced post-exit before any re-entry.

### G1: G0 + Efficiency Ratio Regime Gate
- **Kaufman Efficiency Ratio:** $\text{ER}_{30}(t) = \frac{|\text{close}_t - \text{close}_{t-30}|}{\sum_{i=0}^{29} |\text{close}_{t-i} - \text{close}_{t-i-1}|}$.
- **Regime Filter:** Enter only when $	ext{ER}_{30}(t) > 	ext{median}(	ext{ER}_{30}, 365	ext{ days})$.
- **Exit Engine:** Same ATR trailing stop and cooldown as G0.

### G2: G0 with Causal EMA(5)-Smoothed Breakout Signals

- **Signal Rules:** $S_t > \max(S_{t-20 \dots t-1}) 	ext{ AND } S_t > 	ext{EMA}_{100}(S)$.
- **Execution & Stops:** Fills, stops, ATR calculation, and trailing stops use raw market prices (zero smoothing lag on exits).

## 6. Comprehensive Failure Mode & Structural Risk Analysis

### 1. Funding Drag on Crypto Perpetuals
Perpetual contracts settle funding rates every 8 hours. During extended crypto bull markets, longs consistently pay annualized funding premiums of 10%–40% to shorts. Conversely, during aggressive sell-offs, shorts pay premiums. For trend strategies with multi-week holding horizons, cumulative funding drag erodes gross trade gains substantially. Across both holdout partitions, funding drag represented up to several percentage points of initial capital, demonstrating that backtests ignoring funding severely overstate edge.

### 2. Choppy / Sideways Regimes & Whipsaw Losses
Donchian breakout systems rely on long-tail fat moves to offset inevitable consecutive losses during sideways consolidation.
